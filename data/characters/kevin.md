# Kevin

## The Wishbook Visionary

Kevin is a renowned, highly idiosyncratic photographer. While he is most famous in the public eye for his seasonal work capturing Mall Santas and shooting the iconic, massive catalog for a national department store's "Wishbook," his orbit crossed with the music industry during the 1992 Cydele multimedia project.

**Role:** Professional Photographer  
**Associated Projects:** `stardust-engine` (The Friction Narrative)
